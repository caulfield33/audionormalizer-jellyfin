Audio Normalizer

Builds an additional audio track next to a film: louder dialogue, quieter explosions. The original file is not modified - the player simply gains one more audio option, and you can switch between them freely.

Loudness thresholds

The average level of the finished track. −16 suits watching at home, −23 is the broadcast standard (quieter).
Nothing rises above this. −1.5 is safe on any device.
How much dynamics to keep. An action film usually has 20-25 LU. 9 is comfortable at night, 14 still feels cinematic.
Even audio gains nothing from a second track - only disk space.

Processing

A plain compressor only removes the loud parts, it never makes dialogue more audible. dynaudnorm lifts the quiet passages, which is what actually solves this.

Channels

A plain 5.1 to stereo downmix makes dialogue quieter: on the test material −37.5 LUFS turned into −44.8. The dialogue matrix is not a luxury.
−60 mutes LFE entirely. That is where most of the action-film boom lives.

Track file

Placed in front of the name: AN - Ukrainian 5.1. That is how normalized tracks stand out in the player audio list.
Used when there is nothing to take from the source track. Avoid the words default, forced, foreign, cc, hi, sdh - Jellyfin reads them as flags, not as a name.
Leave at 0. Only needed when a particular client ignores the codec delay and the sound visibly drifts from the picture.
